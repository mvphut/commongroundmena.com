Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information Common Ground ("we," "us") collects on this website and how it's used. We built this site to be simple, and we've tried to keep this policy just as simple.

What we collect

If you submit your email address through the signup form on this site, we collect that email address for the sole purpose of notifying you when Common Ground launches. We don't ask for your name, phone number, or any other personal details on this page.

How your email is handled

The signup form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form service, which delivers your submission to us by email. We don't sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone else, and we don't use it for anything beyond letting you know when we launch.

Cookies and hosting

This site is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which may set a small number of essential cookies for security and performance purposes (for example, to help distinguish real visitors from automated traffic). These cookies don't track you across other websites and aren't used for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email address from our records at any time by writing to hello@commongroundmena.com. We'll remove it promptly.

Changes to this policy

As Common Ground moves from a coming-soon page to a full product, this policy will be updated to reflect how the product actually works. We'll update the date at the top of this page whenever that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to hello@commongroundmena.com.

This policy covers this coming-soon page only and is written in plain language rather than formal legal drafting. It isn't legal advice — before launch, we'd recommend having it reviewed by a lawyer familiar with data protection rules in the markets Common Ground operates in.
